DynamicStage 4.5.646 — whole dial pad fits in the stage (2026-10-05 NZDT)

Symptom (4.5.645): side-to-side fit perfect, but keys far too large; only
7–9, *0#, green call visible (1–6 cut off above the card).

Root cause: DSResizePhoneKeypad measured the fill band in the FULL-DEVICE root
(y≈36 → tab bar). The root is width-filled and bottom-aligned in the card, so
its upper ~half is clipped by the card window. The pad was scaled to fill a band
mostly off-screen (~1.25x), so only the bottom rows landed in view.

Fix (app/Tweak.xm, DSResizePhoneKeypad):
- Visible band = card window converted into root space (accounts for the root
  fill transform), minus a 44pt card-space status-bar strip and room for the
  typed-number LCD, down to 8pt above the tab/quick bar.
- Per-key placement, ONE uniform scale k per key (circles stay round):
  k = min(sx, 1.06*sy), sy = bandH / padH. Falls back to k = sy if the bump
  would poke above the band.
- Column centers keep the 4.5.645 width spread (sx = availW/padW) — the
  side-to-side fit the user approved. Rows compress to fit. Envelope is
  bottom-aligned above the tabs.
- Hidden controls and controls inside the LCD (Add Number) are excluded from
  the pad union.
- LCD (class name contains "LCD") is translated into view just above the pad,
  never under the status-bar strip.
- Fit log line: "app: phone pad fit646 k=… sx=… sy=… vis=… band=… padTop=…".
