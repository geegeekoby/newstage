DynamicStage 4.5.651 - drag rim always wins the touch
=====================================================
Package: com.recreated.dynamicstage_4.5.651_iphoneos-arm64.deb (based on 4.5.650; all 650 fixes kept)
Changed: springboard/DSRimCatcherView.h/.m (new), springboard/DSStageDragShellView.h/.m,
         springboard/DSStageContainerView.h/.m, springboard/DSStageManager.m,
         springboard/DSInCallStage.m, shared/DSConstants.h, control, changelog.md

Symptom
-------
The drag rim around a stage card was hard to grab with some apps in the card,
and over the normal full-screen app outside the stage. On the Home Screen it was
mostly fine.

Root cause
----------
iOS decides which PROCESS gets a touch in the window server, before
SpringBoard's UIKit hit test, the stage window's touchTest or the rim's pan
recognizers ever run. The window server looks at the layers on screen under
the finger. The rim band was see-through: a clear UIView (the drag shell and
the top/float rim views) with only the 3pt outline drawn. A clear SpringBoard
view doesn't count there, so:
 - Outside the card, the touch went to the full-screen app drawn under the
   stage. SpringBoard never saw it, so the rim pan couldn't win no matter what
   its gesture settings were.
 - Just inside the card edge, the hosted app's own scene layer is on top. The
   touch went straight to that app's process. The card's UIKit "claim the
   18pt band" hitTest (4.5.4xx) never ran for those touches.
 - Only a touch right on the drawn 3pt outline landed on a SpringBoard layer.
   That is why it worked "sometimes", and always over the Home Screen
   (SpringBoard draws everything there).
Games / Metal apps are the same case (their layer simply wins).
Gesture priority inside SpringBoard was secondary. The rim pan already begins
after 2pt and doesn't wait for other gestures. System edge gestures were only
held for touches inside the card, not on the rim.

Fix
---
1. DSRimCatcherView: invisible strips that the window server treats as solid.
   They have a 1.1% alpha fill (imperceptible), plus layer hitTestsAsOpaque
   where the iOS build has it. They're laid over the rim band:
   - outer ring, 16pt outside the card edge: in the main card's drag shell
     and in the split / floating cards' rim views;
   - inner ring, 14pt inside the card edge: inside every card, above the
     hosted app view, grips and buttons.
   A strip doesn't do gesture work itself. Its hitTest hands the touch to the
   view whose recognizers already own the rim (drag shell, rim view, or the
   card itself, exactly as the old band check did), so the existing drag /
   resize / park logic is unchanged. A visible card control under the point
   (the + button) keeps its tap.
   The strips sit inside the area the stage window already claims (touchTest:
   card + 36-44pt), so every touch the window server sends to SpringBoard
   there is handled. None is swallowed.
2. Hit-test order: the drag shell and the card check their strips before
   anything else (before the card's hosted scene view).
3. Gesture priority in the stage window: other recognizers that get the same
   rim touch (picker scroll, taps, status peek) now wait for the rim pan to
   fail (gestureRecognizer:shouldBeRequiredToFailByGestureRecognizer:). The
   rim pan never waits for anyone.
4. System gestures (SBSystemGestureManager / SBFluidSwitcherGestureManager
   shouldBegin / shouldReceiveTouch hooks) are held when they start on the
   16pt outer rim ring of a visible card. Before, this only applied inside the
   card.
5. Split cards: a card's outer strips never cover the other split card's
   interior (that stays the other app's touch). The strips re-lay whenever the
   stage geometry changes (placeCard, rim placement, keyboard lift/slide).
6. 650 in-call screen: it's now scaled into the card inset by the 14pt inner
   rim band. The call window is above the stage (999 > 998), so a full-card
   call screen would have covered the inner rim; touches on the band now
   reach the rim.
7. Log (rate limited, at most 4 lines / 2s, deduped), "SpringBoard: rim651 ..."
   in diagnostics:
   - "hit <card>-<inner|outer>-<side> at x,y -> <path>": a strip caught the
     touch. path = DSStageDragShellView / DSStageRimHitView /
     DSStageContainerView (whose pans take it) or card-control.
   - "drag began at x,y won by <main-shell-pan|top-rim-pan|float-rim-pan|
     top-card-pan|float-card-pan|card-pan>"
   - "system gesture held at x,y (rim)"

Not touched / safety
--------------------
- Touches outside the rim aren't taken: strips cover only 16pt outside and 14pt
  inside the edge. The rest of the card is the app's, and the rest of the
  screen is the full-screen app's.
- Home bar (bottom centre 28pt) is cut out of every strip and never claimed.
  The system gesture hold skips the very top edge (y < 10: Notification /
  Control Center) and the bottom 22pt (home, switcher, corner pull).
- Keyboard: SpringBoard's keyboard window (level 1000) stays above the stage.
  Strips are cut above a card's keyboard band.
- No new windows, no window level changes, no scene writes, no layoutIfNeeded:
  the SIGTRAP hardening is unchanged.
- No per-frame work in the switcher: the strips are laid out only when the
  stage geometry changes. The system gesture hold returns early while
  +[DSSceneHost systemTransitionBusy] is set (home / switcher transition).

Limits
------
- Not tested on a device here (this build environment has no phone). The
  log lines above show which path won on the device.
- The 14pt inside the card edge now belongs to the rim. An app's own edge
  gesture that has to start in that strip (e.g. a back swipe starting right at
  the card's left edge) or a tap on a control within 14pt of the card edge is
  now a rim touch. Starting a little further in works as before.
- Windows that are above the stage stay above it on purpose: keyboard,
  alerts, banners, PiP, Dynamic Island / status bar, and the call screen
  inside the card.
- The 16pt outer ring takes that strip from the full-screen app around the
  card while the stage is visible (that is the rim).
- If a later iOS build stops counting near-zero-alpha layers as solid and has
  no hitTestsAsOpaque, the outer band falls back to the old behaviour (outline
  line + Home Screen); the "hit ..." log lines would then be missing for
  touches over apps.
