DynamicStage 4.5.659 - SIGTRAP on corner restore after a home swipe with two stages
==================================================================================
Base: 4.5.658 (all camera658 logging kept).

CRASH (from "Camera debug.txt", newstage repo, crash section)
  build: 4.5.657   when: 1791271444 (Tue Oct 6 20:24:04 NZDT)   signal: SIGTRAP
  Recent stage lines, oldest first. The last three runs before the crash:
    SpringBoard: tucking the open stage for home
    SpringBoard: home sends the two stages to opposite corners
    SpringBoard: org.whispersystems.signal will follow the home transition
    SpringBoard: home gesture began, stage minimized
    ... (three home swipes in a row; "home gesture ended" and
         "parked apps stopped following the home transition" each appear twice)
    SpringBoard: held a minimized app view update so another app can open
    SpringBoard: corner pull picked up from the system gesture
    SpringBoard: restored the right stage, the other stays minimised
    SpringBoard: held a minimized stage scene update so another app can open   (x2)
    SpringBoard: org.whispersystems.signal had been put in the background off the stage and was woken
    SpringBoard: kept the open stage inside its card while another app opened
    === end ===  (SIGTRAP)
- The 4.5.650 crash in "New debug.txt" has the same system-library frames (same boot, so
  the same addresses), and it also follows "corner pull picked up from the system
  gesture". So it is the same crash site, and older than 4.5.657.
- No "switcher657 ... closed outside the stage" line in the ring, so the 657 lazy-kill
  path is not involved. Signal was not killed; it was parked and handed to the home
  transition.

ROOT CAUSE
1. With two stages, a home swipe parks both cards in opposite corners. The parked card's
   app (Signal here, the second stage's app) is set to follow the system home transition:
   automatesLifecycle YES on its SBAppViewController, so iOS drives its lifecycle during
   the swipe. 0.85 s after the swipe ends it is handed back (automatesLifecycle NO, a
   property only). iOS finishes its side of that hand-back after the flip.
2. The minimize-on-home-swipe hold (homeGestureIsActive, the 1.15 s quiet window, and the
   stays-backgrounded hold) only covers the gesture itself. A corner pull right after the
   swipe is stage use: it clears "system owns the screen" and the restore clears
   stays-backgrounded on the card coming back. Then finishCornerRestoreOfCard ->
   setForeground:YES / wakeIfBackgrounded -> activateHostedAppWhenMediaAllows did
   _setCurrentMode:2 + setDisplayMode:4 + _activateApp on Signal's app view ("... was
   woken"), while the hand-back was still settling. Nothing in the wake path knew this
   app view had just been iOS's.
3. iOS answered that activation with a full-screen scene update for Signal. The FBScene
   hook classed it as "another app opened" and refused it: completion(YES) without
   %orig ("kept the open stage inside its card while another app opened"). An
   activation whose own scene update is refused is left half-applied, and SpringBoard
   asserts on it: the SIGTRAP. (The FIX comments already name changing an app view's mode
   on the way back from a home transition as a SIGTRAP; the wake path still did it.)

WHAT CHANGED (springboard/DSSceneHost.m/.h, springboard/Tweak.xm)
- DSSceneHost keeps _systemHomeSettleUntil. It is set to 3.0 s after an app view stops
  following the home transition, and it counts as settling while it follows.
- activateHostedAppWhenMediaAllows: while settling, the wake waits instead of running.
  The retries are merged into one (all three restore paths ask at once), capped at about
  20 tries, then dropped with a log line (tapping the card wakes it as before). After the
  wait it activates, lays the host view out and logs "woken after ... settled".
- Within 4 s of a hand-back, an app view that is already Live (displayMode 4) keeps its
  mode: activation only, no _setCurrentMode / setDisplayMode. Every other wake is
  unchanged.
- An activation made right after a hand-back marks that app as "stage activating" for
  1.5 s. In the FBScene updateSettings:withTransitionContext: hook, a phone-sized update
  for that scene is no longer refused. It goes the normal path, where the card geometry
  override is written over the phone frame and the update is applied (%orig). The
  "kept inside its card" refusal stays for every other app opening.
- wakeIfBackgrounded doesn't log "was woken" for a wake that is only queued.
- Unchanged: the 4.5.657 switcher no-op (nothing new runs in the switcher; the new checks
  are ivar and time reads inside the existing wake path only), the call guard (call653),
  the boot guard, 4.5.656's call-screen hiding, and all 4.5.658 camera658 logging.

NEW LOG LINES ("SpringBoard: home659 ...")
  held the wake of <app> until its home-transition hand-back settles (following=N, X s left)
  <app> woken after its home-transition hand-back settled
  <app> wake dropped, its home-transition hand-back did not settle

TEST
Two stages open. Swipe home two or three times quickly, then pull a minimized stage back
from its corner straight away. Repeat with the other corner. Expected: no safe mode. The
restored card shows its last picture for up to about 3 s, then goes live. The log shows
"home659 held the wake ..." then "home659 ... woken after ... settled". If it still
crashes, send the crash section and the 20 lines before it.
