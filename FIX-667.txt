ROOT CAUSE
Beeper - hypothesis (a) confirmed, (b) partly, (c) addressed:
  (a) The 664 "camera open" rule needs the stage to have seen startRunning on a hooked session
      (running) or a camera-named view controller (screens). Beeper's React Native camera is
      neither visible to us, so the camera layout never engaged: Beeper's phone-tall camera screen
      stayed laid out past the 458pt card and the shutter row was cut off.
  (b) A modally presented camera screen sits in a UITransitionView beside the root; the 663 FILL
      only scaled window roots, so a presented camera screen would not have been scaled either.
  (c) Decided only on events; no periodic look, so a preview arriving later was missed.
  Logging: Beeper / Messenger / Signal cannot open /var/tmp from their sandbox, and none of their
  Darwin-notify reports has ever reached SpringBoard in any log (not even the ctor line). So there
  was no app-side evidence from Beeper at all. (Whether the dylib loads in Beeper could not be
  proven from these logs; 667's relay answers that - see TEST.)
Messages - taps reach the extension (close X works), so this is not hit routing. The camera is
  Apple's (non-injected) camera extension in a 420x458 remote view; it lays its camera out for the
  phone's full height, so the shutter row sits below the card and the lower card is viewfinder.

WHAT CHANGED
app/DSCameraStage.m
  - DSCam667PreviewOnScreen(): a visible AVCaptureVideoPreviewLayer (>= 80x80) covering >= 30% of a
    normal window counts as camera open on its own (never keyboard / text-effects / input /
    above-status-bar windows; never while a keyboard >= 120pt is up - 664 rule kept).
  - Preview watch: once a second on main while staged and not backgrounded (DSCam667PollTick),
    plus all existing event/layout triggers.
  - Modally presented full-card views (presentedViewController chain) get the same FILL +
    bottom-align as the root; restored to their container when the camera layout goes off.
  - Log: "layout667 <bundle> camera layout ENGAGED: FILL scale=... phone=430x932 card=420x458
    roots=n presented=n bottomAlign=1 why=...", then "layout667 <bundle> camera layout geometry:
    ... preview=WxH at x,y = P% of <window>", and "layout667 <bundle> camera layout released (...)".
  - Messages extension camera (any staged app whose camera screen holds a remote view, no keyboard,
    no in-process camera layout): the view holding it gets phone bounds 430x932 and FIT scale
    (0.491) centred in the card, so the extension lays out exactly as on the phone and the whole
    camera (close X top, shutter row bottom) is on the card. UIKit frame/bounds/center/transform
    writes on that one view are dropped while it is held (DSCameraStageHoldsView); restored when
    the camera screen goes or a keyboard comes up. Re-applied every 0.5 s with the 666 tick.
    Log: "layout667 com.apple.MobileSMS remote camera fit ON for CKFunCameraViewController: ...",
    "layout667 ... remote camera after 0.7s: target=... bounds=430x932 scale=0.491 on card=... |
    remote=_UIRemoteView bounds=... on card=...", "layout667 ... remote camera fit off (...)".
app/Tweak.xm
  - UIView setFrame/setBounds/setCenter/setTransform: early drop for the held remote-camera view,
    pass-through for the stage's own write. Nothing else changed (666 hit-test bypass, keyboard
    hooks, Beeper paths untouched).
shared/DSDiagnostics.m/.h, shared/DSConstants.h
  - Trace writer falls back to <app data container>/tmp/com.recreated.dynamicstage.app-trace.log when
    /var/tmp is refused (EPERM/EACCES/EROFS) - sandboxed apps only; Messages/Phone/SpringBoard

RISKS
- Beeper: if the dylib does not load in Beeper at all, no app-side fix can act; the relay lines
  will show that (no "app trace file found" / no "app ctor" line from Beeper).
- A full-window video-call self-preview (>= 30% of the window) in Messenger/Signal/Beeper would
  also get the camera FILL layout while no keyboard is up.
- Messages camera is FIT (scaled ~0.49, black side bars) - small but whole. Touches into a scaled
  remote view rely on the window server mapping through the layer transform; tap666 + layout667
  lines will show if that fails.
- While held, the Messages camera view ignores UIKit frame changes (dismiss animation may not slide).
