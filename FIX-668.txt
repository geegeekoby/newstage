ROOT CAUSES
1. SIGTRAP: the FBScene updateSettings hook still REFUSED SpringBoard's phone-size update of a hosted app
   (completion done(YES) with no %orig) outside the 659/665 exemptions. SpringBoard then believes the
   update landed while the scene never got it; its next settings diff for that app view does not match
   and the original app-view handler asserts. Not the 661/667 display-layout republish, not the camera fit
   (the trap frame is the app-view settings handler, not the arbiter or the camera code).
2. Beeper: DynamicStageApp.dylib is not running inside Beeper (no ctor / trace / hello ever, from any
   build, while the filter plist does list com.beeper.chat.ios and the arm64 slice is valid and signed).
   Most likely Beeper is excluded from injection on the phone (jailbreak-hiding / tweak-blocking for that
   app). Cannot be proven from the box; 668 adds markers that prove it either way. So the 667 in-app camera
   fit never ran.
3. SpringBoard could not see who used the camera: STListData not unwrapped (items=0).
4. Messages: 667 FIT the whole phone-size extension layout (scale 0.49) -> narrow centred column.
