1) The two SIGTRAP crashes in Crashsnstuff.txt (build 4.5.673)
   when 1791454150 = 2026-10-08 23:09:10 NZDT (ring printed twice, lines ~135 and ~560)
   when 1791456413 = 2026-10-08 23:46:53 NZDT (line ~192)
   Both backtraces: frames 0/1 0x221403214 / 0x203dd3fa8 (SpringBoard / FrontBoard, same as
   the 667/669 traps), frame 2 in DynamicStage.dylib at an address ending ...205c / ...605c
   (= 0x205c mod 0x4000, the 16 KB page slide keeps the low 14 bits). In the 4.5.673 arm64e
   slice (UUID E101414A-..., kept in dsym673/) the only call whose return address fits is
   0xa058 (a PAC'd BLRAA) -> llvm-symbolizer:
     _logos_method$Stage$SBAppViewController$sceneHandle$didUpdateSettingsWithDiff$previousSettings$
     springboard/Tweak.xm:764   (the @try { %orig; } of the owned app view)
   So: SpringBoard's own SBAppViewController settings handler traps on an update of
   Beeper's Live card (same frame as 667/669).
   What is new in both recent-line rings: right before the trap Beeper's card was put back in
   the display layout as "role=6 level=1050 template=1" (after a corner pull / coming back
   from minimized), and the next app-view diff (statusBarStyleOverridesToSuppress /
   targetOfEventDeferringEnvironments) went to %orig and trapped. Every earlier log (667-673)
   and every 4.5.673 session without that template published role=1 level=1 template=0 and
   never crashed there. The stage learns its element "template" from SpringBoard's own
   addElement calls; it had copied a full-screen system element (role 6, level 1050 - the
   ring shows a full-screen camera stop just before: "fff66709 stop from an app that is not
   on a card, full-screen camera left alone").
   Fix:
   - Only an ordinary primary app element (role 1, level 0..10) is used as template; anything
     else is ignored ("presence674 ignored SpringBoard's full-screen element role=.. level=..").
   - Every publish (also a re-publish after minimize / park / corner pull) now starts the
     1 s hold of the Live app view's SpringBoard updates (before: only moves / removals).
   dSYMs: dsym673/ (673 obj incl. .dSYM, UUID E101414A-9D6E-35DC-8926-B73D7DC0F173 arm64e)
          dsym674/ (674, UUID E693E20F-6C4A-3593-ABE0-3626D07AA230 arm64e). Kept per build.
