WHY 680-683 FAILED
- Beeper (no app dylib on NathanLR) gets nothing back from the keyboard arbiter. The only things
  SpringBoard can steer are its scene settings: frame, safe area and orientation.
- 680-682: in the 430x931 scene Beeper moved its bar 897 -> 586 inside its own process, and the
  card's window chased it from SpringBoard: two processes racing, hence the bounce.
- 683: keys inside the card; the user reports it doesn't work. Now opt-in only.

WHAT 684 DOES (allowlisted apps without the dylib = Beeper)
1. Same always-full-screen scene {{0,0},{430,931}} at x0.9767, but safe area {T, 0, K, 0}:
     K = 931 - 586 = 345  (Beeper's 346 pt keyboard at the screen bottom, top 586)
     T = ceil(586 - cardH/fit) = 117 on a 420x458 card (Beeper's top on the card's top edge)
   Keys down: Beeper rests its bar above the K inset   -> bar bottom 586.
   Keys up:   Beeper puts its bar on the keyboard top  -> bar bottom 586. Same y, nothing to move.
   (675/680: Beeper put the bar ON the keyboard top, not keyboard + safe area, and followed the
   34 pt inset with the keys down; the 677 log reads the safe area back on the scene.)
2. The card's window is FIXED at app y 117..586, keyboard up or down. phoneSize682MoveBarBottomTo,
   the 681 spring, the 682 instant move, the keys674 trigger and its revert are all skipped
   ("phonesize684 ... skipped" lines).
3. keys674 answers NO again, so Beeper's keys show full size at the bottom of the phone
   (SpringBoard's keyboard host, as in 674-682); taps go straight to Beeper.
4. K is learned: during a typing session, the stage's keyboard top that was up the longest (at
   least 150 ms) sets K = 931 - top (clamped 200..500). A changed K, tune file or off file is
   written only once the keys have been down 1.2 s (retried while the stage still holds keys, a
   scene update runs or a card is dragged), with the same guarded geometry write and app-view hold
   as a card resize. Learned values last until a respring.
5. Card resize: T is recomputed in the existing resize write; the pin stays 931-K.

WHAT THE USER SHOULD SEE
Beeper's card: the header at the top, the bar on the bottom edge. Tap the message box: the keys
come up full size at the bottom of the phone, outside the card; the card and Beeper's bar don't
move at all. Hide: the keys go down; nothing in the card moves. Below the bar Beeper has a
345 pt empty strip and above the header a 117 pt strip; both are off the card.

                                                  1.2 s after the keys go down)
  /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize675-off   -> 4.5.674 (card-size scene, keys at the phone bottom)
  /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize683-on    -> 4.5.683 mode (keys inside the card), opt-in
  /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize684-tune  -> key=value lines:
      bottom=100..600|auto   K override (auto: learned, default 345)
      top=0..400|auto        top inset (auto: the card's top edge, 117)
      mode=safe|short        short: scene 430x(931-K) ending at the keyboard top, no bottom inset
      Recipes: bar sits ~N pt too low with keys up  -> bottom=345+N
               bar sits ~N pt too high              -> bottom=345-N
               header cut / gap at the top          -> top=<value>
               try the no-overlap variant           -> mode=short

RISKS
- If Beeper stacks the safe area and the keyboard (it didn't in 675/680), the bar would sit 345 pt
  above the keys with the keyboard up: use the off file.
- Two-step keyboard (301 then 346, ~8-25 ms apart): the bar may dip a few pt for a frame.
- Other keyboard heights (emoji, no suggestion bar): the first time the bar can move by the
  difference; K is re-learned and applied with the keys down. A one-off 586->631 flicker isn't
  learned (the longest-held top wins).
- 345 pt of Beeper's own background under the bar and 117 pt above the header are off-card; any
  Beeper UI drawn there (e.g. a panel sized to the safe area) won't be seen.
- A bottom-half card is still lifted above the keys by the stage (the whole card moves, the bar
  doesn't move inside it).
- Not tested on a phone.
