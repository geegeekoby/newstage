bottom (screen 586..932), and the card is at screen 5..463. The bar only has to stay still. With
the scene 931 tall and a resting bottom inset B, the bar stays still with the keys up when

  B >= keyboard height (346, or 301)            (an app's own "keyboard height - inset" math).
