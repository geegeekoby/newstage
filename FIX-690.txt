DynamicStage 4.5.690 - Beeper top bar recreated on the card (SpringBoard-side picture)

User (689 good): "now all you have to do is move top bar of beeper down a little bit .. even if you
recreate it ... dont change how keyboard or anything works... just the top bar"

Uploads: newstage origin/main = 48ab94a (our 689). No new upload, so there was no 688 crash log to decode.

Constraint kept: the 684/689 geometry is byte for byte the same. The diff vs 689 is additions only in
springboard/DSSceneHost.m (plus the version bump). The host view placement code in layoutHostView is untouched,
and nothing writes to the scene settings. Tweak.xm, DSKeys674.m and DSStageManager.m are unchanged.

What 690 adds (DSSceneHost.m):
- DSPhoneSize690StripView: a clipping view inserted into the card's contentView just above the host
  view, frame (0, place_y, cardWidth, ceil(strip_height*fit)), filled with the card background colour.
- Inside it a _UIPortalView with sourceView = the card's host view. matchesPosition, matchesTransform,
  matchesAlpha, hidesSourceView and forwardsClientHitTesting are all NO (the 673 camera recipe).
  bounds = scene 430x931, transform = scale(fit), center = (cardW/2, (931/2 - strip_top)*fit), so app
  y strip_top..strip_top+strip_height shows at card y place_y.. at the same x0.977 and x position as the card.
- Made and laid out from layoutHostView's phone-size branch only, after the host is placed, wrapped in
  @try. It's only made with the 684 geometry (not with the 685/687/688-geo opt-ins), when the strip is
  above the card's visible top (117), and on the main thread. It's removed when the card stops being
  phone-size, is minimized, is let go or torn down, or when off/on=0/the latch applies.
- Touch: forward (default) sets setAllowsHitTesting:YES on the portal, so the window server can route a
  tap through the portal to Beeper's real top bar. The strip is filled and its UIKit hitTest returns self,
  so a tap that is not routed is kept by SpringBoard (never reaches the chat under the strip) and logged:
  "phonesize690 ... touch on the recreated top bar reached SpringBoard at strip x,y (app x,y)".
  block: portal hit testing off, the strip keeps all taps. pass: clear background, no interaction, taps go
  to the content under the strip.
- Logs: phonesize690 tune ..., top-bar strip made/updated (app y range, card y range, scale, the card's
  own unchanged range), no top-bar strip (reason), strip removed (reason), crash latch lines.

Files:
- Tune: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize690-tune
    strip_top=59      app y where the strip starts (0..400)
    strip_height=68   app pt (10..200)
    place_y=0         card y of the strip's top (-50..300; e.g. 10 = 10 pt lower on the card)
    on=1|0
    touch=forward|block|pass
- Off: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize690-off = no strip = exact 4.5.689.
- Latch: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize690-trying (15 s), -crashed (delete it to allow the strip again).

Taps: unverified on device. The 4.5.673 camera work found that portal mirrors don't get touches, because
the window server routes by context at the touch position. The CAPortalLayer allowsHitTesting route is the
one remaining possibility. If it doesn't work, the log will show the touch reached SpringBoard, and the
buttons are dead but safe (nothing under them is triggered).

Risks: the portal of a live layer-host subtree (the 673 camera did the same, so it's known to work on
iOS 16). The blur in the top bar may look slightly different inside the portal. The strip covers the first
~66 pt of the card (app y 117..185 of the real content). The crash latch auto-disables the strip after a
SpringBoard crash within 15 s.
