DynamicStage 4.5.691 - content below the recreated top bar (top safe-area inset only)

User on 690: "thats way better but the inside contents are cut off by the top bar maybe shift them into
view or fit everything nicely". The 690 strip (app y 59..127 at card y 0..67) covers app y ~118..186 of the
real content.

Uploads: newstage origin/main = 22c3ca2 (our 690). No new upload.

Evidence (uploads685 photo + log, 684 geometry): the log shows the scene safe area {118,0,345,0}, and the
card shows app y ~117..586. Beeper's top bar sat at 59..127, ignoring the 118 top inset (only its 10 pt
bottom band showed at the card top). The main-screen grid's first row starts about 30 pt below the bar
(app y ~158), so content is laid out from the bar, not from 118 + bar height. That 118 is above the
content's top (127), so a view below the bar sees no top safe area at all (scroll view safe area = overlap).
With a top inset of 186, any Beeper view that respects its safe area (automatic scroll insets or safe-area
padding) gets ~59 pt of top inset, which is exactly the strip.

Options evaluated:
(a) raise only the top inset to the strip's bottom: chosen as the default (top_inset=auto -> 186).
    Same validated 684 write path (DSApplyGeometry: finite, 0..2000, setter only if it responds), only
    with the keys down and the card at rest, the same timing as 684. Frame, bottom inset 345 and bar line
    586 are unchanged, so it's safe for the chat bar: Beeper puts the bar on the keyboard top (keys up) or
    on the bottom inset (keys down), never on the top inset. The safe area height goes from 468 to 400 pt.
    It's not proven that Beeper honours it. If it doesn't, nothing changes (it looks like 690) and option
    (b) is the fallback.
(b) slimmer strip: strip=slim = app y 59..119 (60 pt). It drops the empty blur band 119..127 that the 684
    photo showed under the buttons, saving ~8 pt. Any strip_top / strip_height works too.
(c) other SpringBoard-only options without scaling: the card shows a fixed 469 pt of app height ending at
    586. Bar + content = 68 + 459 pt > 469, so something has to go. Mirroring content lower would cover the
    real bottom (the chat bar must stay the live, touchable view). Not shipped: no lossless visual option
    exists. A translucent strip was rejected as unreadable.

Code (DSSceneHost.m only, plus the version):
- DSPhoneSize691Off / DSPhoneSize691CurrentTune (top_inset) / latch (DSPhoneSize691Latched,
  DSPhoneSize691MarkTrying).
- The 690 tune reader also reads the 691 tune file (after the 690 file, so it wins, unless 691 is off).
  New key strip=slim|full.
- ivars _ps691Mode/_ps691Value/_ps691StripH/_ps691PlaceY, set in phoneSize684Configure and the
  apply-at-rest step (a change there triggers the normal 684 geometry write).
- phoneSize684Insets: only in the default 684 geometry (not 685/687/688-geo, and only when the 684 tune's
  top is auto), top = ceil(684 auto top + place_y/fit + strip_height) (= 118 + 68 = 186), or the fixed value,
  clamped to pin-100. Bottom stays phone height - pin.
- auto requires the strip to be on (not 690-off / on=0 / 690 latch). A fixed top_inset=N works without
  the strip.
- Logs: phonesize691 tune ..., phonesize691 <app> top inset A -> B, the 684 summary ", 691: top inset raised
  to 186 ...", and the latch lines.

Files:
- Tune: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize691-tune
    top_inset=auto | off | 20..480
    strip=slim | full
    strip_top=, strip_height=, place_y=, on=1|0, touch=forward|block|pass  (override the 690 tune)
- Off: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize691-off = exact 4.5.690.
- Latch: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-phonesize691-trying (15 s), -crashed (delete it to allow again).

Applies at the next card open, or 1 s after the keyboard goes down with the card at rest (684 timing).
