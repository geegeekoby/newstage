DynamicStage 4.5.692 - Beeper settings button back on the recreated top bar

User on 691: "okay perfect only problem now is setting button in the top bar is missing".
Uploads: newstage origin/main = 4455f38 (our 691). No screenshot or log.

CAUSE (most likely; not proven without a screenshot)
- 690 to 691 changed exactly one thing: the scene's top safe-area inset, 118 to 186. The strip still
  copies app y 59..127, and the strip itself was not changed.
- Beeper's bar background and title don't follow the top inset. With 118 the bar stayed at 59..127
  (684 photo: only its 10 pt bottom band showed at the card top, nothing else).
- Beeper's content does follow it: 691's 186 moved the content below the strip, which the user confirmed.
- Beeper's help says settings is behind the top-left icon of the chat list (the inbox menu). A button
  that follows the safe-area top (instead of the static bar) moved down by the same 186 - 118 = 68 pt in
  691, to about app y 127..186. That is exactly the area the strip covers on the card (app y ~118..186),
  so it's under the strip and outside the strip's copied range. This also fits 684, where it would sit
  above the card's top (117) and be invisible.
- Ruled out: the card's rounded corner (radius 55 still leaves a button at x 16..52 / y 75..111 fully
  inside); the strip's x mapping (same as the host's). A portal dropping a sublayer is unlikely, since
  only the inset changed between 690 and 691.

FIX (DSSceneHost.m only, plus the version; additions only vs 691)
- A "patch" inside the 690 strip (tag 69201, no interaction) holds a second _UIPortalView of the same
  host view. It copies app y (strip_top + shift)..min(strip_top + strip_height + shift, top inset)
  = 127..186, for app x 0..100, and draws it at strip y 0..~58 (the button's old place) at the same x and
  scale.
- The source is cut at the top inset (186) so no Beeper content is ever copied.
- Blend: lighten (dark mode) / darken (light mode) via layer.compositingFilter (only if the layer
  responds). The band's black background leaves the bar as it was; only the lighter button pixels show.
  If nothing is there, the bar looks like 691.
- Taps: in forward mode the patch's portal hit-tests too, so a tap could route to the button's real
  (moved) place. If it doesn't, the strip keeps the tap as in 690/691 (logged).
- Made and laid out from the existing 690 strip update (card layout), main thread, inside @try. Only
  when 691's raised inset is active (shift = 186 - 118 = 68), unless patch_shift= is set.

Files

    patch=on|off       (on)

RISKS
- If the cause is wrong, the patch copies only black, so with lighten nothing changes (same as 691).
- While the chat list is scrolled up, rows passing under the strip's top-left 100 pt may show faintly
  there (lighten). Narrow it with patch_w, or set patch=off.
- The button glyph is drawn without the bar's background behind it (blended onto the bar instead).

KEYBOARD DELAY (secondary)
Nothing of ours delays Beeper's keys:
- keys674 (_canShowKeyboardLayer) is a synchronous yes/no with no timer.
- The _UIKeyboardArbiter updateKeyboardStatus hook calls %orig synchronously. Our work is dispatched
  async after it.
- For 684 apps, phoneSize680KeyboardSignal -> phoneSize684ArbiterOn only logs and records times
  (window fixed, no scene write, no animation).
- _UIRemoteKeyboards addHostedWindowView leaves out only the in-card copy of the keyboard view (the
  band keys674 prevents); the keys at the phone bottom are SpringBoard's own keyboard host and aren't
  touched.
- The only timer near the keys is DSRaiseKeyboardWindowAboveStage's 0.12 s retries during a scene
  update. Those change the window level only (the keys at 586..932 don't overlap the card at 5..463).
  Not changed, since it isn't a show delay and touching it could break the keys elsewhere.
Any visible lag is most likely iOS handing keyboard focus to an app that isn't frontmost (the remote
keyboard round trip) plus Beeper's own animation.
