TOUCHES
The card shows Beeper through SpringBoard's scene layer host. Touches on it go to Beeper by the window
server, which hit-tests the hosted context and converts the point with the inverse of the layer's full
transform chain. That's what made taps land right with 684's x0.977 on a 430-wide scene. A non-uniform
scale is still an affine transform (invertible, same code path), so a tap at card (x, y) should reach app
(x/0.9767, 59 + y/0.8691). The SpringBoard side doesn't change (the card's hitTest is unchanged, and no
strip view sits on top now). Not verified on the device; if taps land vertically off, sy= or
mode=691 shows it quickly.

screen or chat down to the bar or bottom sheet at the card bottom, everything ~11% shorter than wide
