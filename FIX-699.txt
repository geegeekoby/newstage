Cause: Beeper minimised, phone locked a while -> iOS put the scene in the background. The card was brought back
  but stayed backgrounded (black; no wake: setStaysBackgrounded:NO only re-registers geometry, and the 696
  watchdog needs two quiet 1.5 s ticks with no drag). The user dragged the black card from the rim; Live
  (mode 4) updates for a backgrounded scene with no deactivation reasons were not held by any 670/696 hold
  (lock hold ended at unlock; drag hold covers only 1.2 s settle windows, and its log is throttled) and went
  to SpringBoard's handler, which traps (SIGTRAP; @try cannot catch it) -> safe mode.
  Uncertain: the exact update that trapped is not in the ring (hold/diff logs were throttled); whether it was
  during the drag or just after the settle window is unknown. Backgrounded-scene is inferred from the black card
  plus the 696 Blackout log pattern (fg NO after lock).
