Cause (code): minimizeCard:toCornerAnimated: puts the card in its park spot at once and runs its completion 0.30 s later
  (_state = Minimized, hideParkedCardsCompletely, giveBackKeyWindow, setForegroundForAllHostedApps:NO, ...). The completion
  had no idea whether it was still wanted. A corner pull picks the card up as soon as it is parked; a fast restore
  (setParked:NO, state Overlay) finished before that completion, and the stale completion then set the stage to Minimized
  and backgrounded the app under a restored, un-parked card. Un-parked = no corner icon and parkedCardForCornerPoint
  returns nil (no corner pull); Minimized = layout puts the card in the park spot. Stuck until a home swipe re-parks it.
  The reverse also existed: a restore's reveal (finishCornerRestoreOfCard, 0.26-0.45 s later, re-tried every 0.3 s during
  the home gesture) and the open spring's completion ran even if the card was minimised again meanwhile. 698 made the first
  race easier to hit: its flight puts the real card in the corner at once, so the pull can catch it immediately (697 moved
  the real card for 0.32 s).
