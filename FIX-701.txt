User ask: "check for what could cause battery drain or if we could prevent battery drain without ruining the background part fully"

Audit (SpringBoard + app dylibs), ranked by likely drain:
 1. App stage probe (app/DSStageContext.m scheduleProbe) - in EVERY injected app (Messages, Messenger, Signal, Beeper, Phone)
    for the process's life: every 0.35 s when not on a card (normal Messages use = ~3 wake-ups/s), every 1.5 s on a card,
    also while backgrounded (minimised Beeper is kept running). Each run reads notify state (and a plist when notify has none).
    -> FIXED: background 6 s; not on a card 1 s (0.35 s for 10 s after launch/foreground); on a card + foreground 1.5 s (same).
       Foreground/active notifications refresh at once. Stage notifications (the real signal) unchanged.
 2. App camera-preview watch (app/DSCameraStage.m DSCam667) - 1 s GCD timer in every injected app except Phone, whole life.
    -> FIXED: 4 s (2 s leeway) when not on a card or in background; back to 1 s at once on foreground or stage notification;
       1 s on a card in the foreground, and while the camera layout is tall (same as before).
 3. SpringBoard stall watchdog (shared/DSDiagnostics.m) - 0.5 s GCD timer forever, screen off included.
    -> FIXED: 1 s unlocked, 10 s (5 s leeway) locked (no stall checks while locked); main thread asleep waiting for work is
       not a stall (After/BeforeWaiting observer) - needed because the stage tick no longer wakes main every 1.5 s.
 4. SpringBoard stage tick (DSStageManager blackout696ScheduleTick: blackout 696, blackout 699 unlock, heal700, perf summary)
    - 1.5 s forever. -> FIXED: 6 s while locked or nothing on the stage (no host, nothing parked/stashed, Closed);
       the lock-state notification runs it 0.3 s and 1.2 s after unlock; 1.5 s with a card staged/minimised and unlocked.
 5. App-trace relay (springboard/DSAppTraceRelay.m) - 1.5 s timer forever (698 skipped 3 of 4 polls when idle, but still woke).
    -> FIXED: timer 6 s (2 s leeway) when no relay app is on a card; locked: no polling, 30 s check; 1.5 s when hosted.
 6. Camera arbiter presence timer (1 s, tolerance 0.3) - only while a card is visible or a staged camera is live, stops otherwise.
    LEFT ALONE (camera/presence path; already stops when idle).
 7. Running assertion keeping a staged/minimised app alive (DSSceneHost holdRunningAssertion, 695 keep-alive) - the largest
    inherent cost (Beeper keeps running instead of being suspended) but it IS the background feature. LEFT ALONE.
 8. In-call watch 0.5 s (only during a call with a card), intro demo 4.4 s / intro watchdog 30 s (only while the intro shows),
    perf698 frame meter CADisplayLink (only with the perf log file). LEFT ALONE (not idle costs).
 9. Switch files: access() at most every 2 s per switch when used (cached). LEFT ALONE.
 Not changed: keyboard, Messages camera (673), app switcher, status bar, hooks (none added), scene foreground/background policy.

Switch: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-battery701-off (crash latch battery701-trying/-crashed, marked when the stage tick first
  slows). The app dylibs can't read that file from their sandbox: SpringBoard publishes notify state of
  com.recreated.dynamicstage.battery701 (1 on, 2 off); apps treat "no state" as on.

Risks: lock detection uses the system Darwin notification com.apple.springboard.lockstate (state != 0 = locked); if it ever
  reads wrong, the stall/relay timers just stay at their unlocked cadence. A missed stage notification in an app that is not on
  a card is now caught within 1 s instead of 0.35 s (after the first 10 s in the foreground). Camera-preview detection for a
  background app moving onto a card relies on the foreground/stage notifications to go back to 1 s (else up to 4 s).
  STALL lines: sampled every 1 s instead of 0.5 s.
