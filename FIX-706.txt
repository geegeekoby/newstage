1. Card stuck after a drag (drag706)
------------------------------------
Causes, found in the code:
 a) handleStagePan keeps the drag in static state shared by every pan (main
    shell, card, top card, top rim, float rim, float card). A second pan
    beginning while one card was held reset "dragging" and _stageDragActive,
    so the held card's moves and release were ignored and it stayed under
    where the finger left it. The other pan's end also let go of drag702's
    hold on the held card.
 b) A drag whose end never arrived left _stageDragActive set, and the
    layout skips placing cards while that is set.
 c) A keyboard side shift or lift with no keyboard left: heal700 (705) no
    longer counts shifted cards, so nothing put them back.
 d) Stored keyboard resting frames survived a swap ("phonesize696 fit: slot 0
    stored resting frame y=469 ... was stale; the card rests at y=5"), so the
    next lift used the pre-drag place.
Fix:
 - One pan owns the drag. Any other pan is ignored until it ends (logged once).
 - Watchdog 0.6 s after every drag end, cancel or fail, and on the stage tick.
   If no finger is down, nothing is animating or settling (spam700, anim702,
   corner restore, home or system gesture, scene update) and a card is not at
   a valid rest for more than 0.5 s, it is settled:
     - keyboard shift with no keyboard -> shift dropped, lift sprung to 0
     - not at its layout place (>3 pt) -> spring back to the layout + stacking order
     - single free card less than 90% on screen -> clamped on screen
     - drag that never ended -> layout unblocked
   Log: "drag706 settled <app> from <drag end|tick|recheck> reason <...>".
   At most two settles per drag, then "drag706 gave up".
 - After a drag with no keyboard anywhere, stale stored resting frames are dropped.

2. Card minimised by itself while dragging (Forcefully-minimised log)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Evidence: 18:48:40.604 "rim651 drag began at 15,482 won by main-shell-pan"
-> 18:48:41.761 Beeper "taken out of the display layout (... minimized)";
18:50:54.438 "drag began at 9,472" -> 18:50:56.224 minimised. Beeper had to
be pulled back from the corner each time. The final state is Beeper parked
in the corner ({{466, 960}, {56, 56}}).
Cause: at release, velocityIsQuickCornerSwipe only looked at the finger's
release speed (down at least 700 pt/s, sideways at least 320 pt/s). Lifting a
finger off the glass often ends with exactly that flick, even after the card
was carried up or only nudged. A corner flick also beat swap and settle.
Fix: a flick only minimises if the card travelled at least 48 pt down during
the drag and the flick, projected 0.25 s ahead, carries the finger into the
lower 38% of the screen on that corner's side. Ending with the finger at the
corner still minimises as before. Logged: "drag706 <app> minimised by the
drag: ..." and "drag706 ... is not a corner swipe: the card settles".

3. Saferestmode crash (4.5.704 SIGTRAP)
---------------------------------------
Cause: the same SpringBoard Live app-view %orig trap as 670/699. It came
right after a corner restore grew the card, when no hold was active (the
drag hold covers card drags only).
Fix: while a corner pull or restore runs, and for 1.5 s after, the Live
app-view update is held ("appview670 held a Live app view update (corner
restore (706))").

5. Battery and performance (perf706)
------------------------------------
 - Stage tick: 30 s while locked (was 6 s, 80% fewer wakeups) and 15 s with
   nothing on the stage (was 6 s, 60% fewer). Opening a stage or unlocking
   runs a tick within 0.3 s, and the sleeping tick is dropped. Counter: slowTick.
 - Per drag frame (60-120 Hz), no longer done:
     - re-raising the card / rim / float / shelf in the view tree (up to 4
       sublayer reorders a frame)
     - the drag-tile reset (6 view property writes)
     - the hidden drag ghost CATransaction
     - the dead corner and terminate hint maths
   Counter: dragSkip.
 - Trace lines are coalesced: one file write per burst instead of one per
   line. Counter: traceFlush.
 - Already true (checked): minimised cards are hidden completely, and the
   camera timer and frame meter only run when needed.

6. Polish (polish706)
---------------------
 - Held lift: the card's shadow grows while held (opacity 0.16 -> 0.24,
   radius 18 -> 26) and settles on release. The card size is never changed.
   Uses the existing haptics.
 - Top-edge rubber band while dragging a card past the top of the screen.
 - Single-card half choice uses the release velocity (iOS PiP projection).
 - Not done (unsafe without new hooks): card scale, a corner icon press state
   (the corner pull comes from the system edge gesture), and animated z-order.

Switches
--------
/var/mobile/.dynamicstage-drag706-off, -perf706-off, -polish706-off
(crash latches <name>-trying / <name>-crashed). With all three off, the build
behaves like 705, apart from the fixed tune log line.
