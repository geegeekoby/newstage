   Not pinned down from the code. Checked 4.5.697 to 4.5.713:

   - The last uploaded log (4.5.706) shows Messages' scene at the card size,
     {{0,0},{420,458}}, safe area {0,0,0,0}: as expected.
   So no build in that range has a visible Messages-specific cause.

   New, read-only: msgdiag714. While a stage is open with Messages on a card,
   one line "msgdiag714 Messages geometry ..." (on change, else once a minute):
   card frame/bounds, lift, side, clip, the hosted view's frame and transform
   inside the card, the scene frame and safe area (and what the stage wants),
   the app picture's size. Writes nothing to any view, layer or scene.
   Off: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-msgdiag714-off
   (crash latch msgdiag714-trying / msgdiag714-crashed).
   Please open Messages on a card, wait 10 s, and upload the log.
