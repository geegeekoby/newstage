1. BLACK BEEPER CARD after lock while minimised (the bug)

 00:50:38 "scene update begin sceneID:com.beeper.chat.ios-default fg=0": the lock put the
          minimised scene in the background (Beeper's 695 keep-while-minimised path leaves it
          open to SpringBoard's updates).
 00:57:03.542 corner restore; 00:57:03.875 "scene update ... fg=1" (SpringBoard bringing it
          forward); 00:57:03.901 "blackout699 ... scene was in the background on a visible
          card: woken" - i.e. the fg=1 update had NOT taken.
 00:57:09-00:57:16 every drag/tap: "scene update ... fg=1" then "had been put in the
          background off the stage and was woken" (x12) - still background each time.
 00:57:18 user closed the card ("app view torn down") and opened a new stage.
 Cause: FBScene update hooks (Tweak.xm) call keepCommittedForegroundOfScene for an
 on-screen hosted scene: the incoming foreground flag is overwritten with the scene's
 committed one ("an on-screen stage keeps the foreground it already has"). Committed =
 background after the lock, so every foreground update - including the ones produced by
 the stage's own wake (_activateApp, used by blackout699 / blackout696 / card tap) - was
 written back to background. The wake could never take; the card stayed black.
 Fix (blackout716, DSSceneHost.m, +41 lines, no new hook): for 2 s after the stage's own
 wake activates an app, a background -> foreground update of that app's scene lands.
 Never foreground -> background (the original purpose of the rule is kept), never
 Messages (com.apple.MobileSMS is never put in the window), never while the phone is
 locked. The Live app view still does not see the flip (crash699 / 668 holds unchanged).
 Log: "blackout716 <scene> woken by the stage: its foreground update lands".
 Off: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-blackout716-off (latch blackout716-trying / -crashed)
