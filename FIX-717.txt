DynamicStage 4.5.717 - bg717: true backgrounding for staged and minimised apps
==============================================================================

What it does
- While an app is on a stage card, minimised to the corner, or stashed next to
  Split, SpringBoard holds a RunningBoard assertion on that app's process:
    RBSAssertion  target = [RBSTarget targetWithPid:<pid>]
                  attributes = @[ [RBSDomainAttribute attributeWithDomain:@"com.apple.frontboard" name:@"Visibility"] ]
                  -initWithExplanation:target:attributes: then -acquireWithError:
  This is the same domain/name FrontBoard (inside SpringBoard) puts on an app
  whose scene is visible (runningboardd logs show "FBApplicationProcess" with
  <RBSDomainAttribute domain:"com.apple.frontboard" name:"Visibility">). While
  it is held RunningBoard treats the process as visible, so it is not
  suspended - not when the card is minimised, not when the phone is locked.
- Released ([assertion invalidate]) when the card closes, the app is replaced
  or leaves the stage (relinquish / tear-down / dealloc of the scene host).
- Re-acquired: on every hold path the stage already had (open, minimise,
  home hand-back, staysBackgrounded) and from the stage tick (<=1.5 s unlocked,
  <=30 s locked, and at once on unlock via the existing unlock listener):
  a relaunched app (new pid) or an assertion RunningBoard dropped is taken
  again. After a respring every assertion dies with SpringBoard; the stage
  rehosts its apps and takes them again.
- The 4.5.6xx BKSProcessAssertion hold is unchanged and still runs alongside.
- Log: "bg717 holding <bundle> pid <n>", "bg717 released <bundle> pid <n> (<why>)",
  "bg717 could not hold ... (<RunningBoard error>)", "bg717 RunningBoard API missing".

How it was verified (build side)
- iPhoneOS16.5 SDK: RunningBoardServices.tbd exports RBSAssertion, RBSTarget,
  RBSDomainAttribute (classes). Selectors are the ones WebKit's
  ProcessAssertionCocoa.mm uses against the same framework (targetWithPid:,
  attributeWithDomain:name:, initWithExplanation:target:attributes:,
  acquireWithError:, invalidate, isValid).
- At runtime: objc_getClass for all three classes, respondsToSelector /
  instancesRespondToSelector for every selector. Anything missing -> one log
  line, nothing held (same as 4.5.716). acquireWithError: returning NO (e.g.
  RunningBoard refusing the attribute) -> log the error, invalidate, do not retry
  for that pid. No linking to RunningBoardServices (all calls via objc_msgSend).
- No new hooks (hook count identical to 4.5.716). Nothing new at load: the
  first bg717 code runs when a card hosts an app.

Scope
- Every staged / minimised / stashed app except Messages (com.apple.MobileSMS),
  which keeps exactly its 4.5.714-716 behaviour. Beeper 684, the camera 673,
  the keyboard and the app switcher are untouched. Scene foreground/background
  state is not changed by bg717 (process lifetime only).

Battery
- A held app runs like a foreground app with the screen off: its timers,
  network sockets, sync loops and any animation it keeps ticking keep running,
  with no 30 s background limit, for as long as it is on the stage or minimised.
  Cost depends on the app: a quiet chat app (Beeper idle on its socket) is small
  (a few % per night); an app that polls, plays video or animates can cost as
  much as leaving it open on screen minus the display. Close the card (or swipe
  it off the stage) to drop the hold.

Off: touch /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-bg717-off (respring not required; the next
tick releases the holds). Crash latch: bg717-trying is written at first use;
a SpringBoard crash within 15 s turns it into bg717-crashed and bg717 stays off
until that file is deleted.

Risks
- "Visibility" is SpringBoard/FrontBoard's own attribute; RunningBoard could
  refuse it for an extra originator assertion -> "bg717 could not hold" in the
  log and behaviour as 4.5.716 (no crash path).
- Jetsam: a visible-priority app is less likely to be killed under memory
  pressure, so other background apps may be evicted sooner.
- Thermal / battery as above.
