DynamicStage 4.5.725 - sync725: typing card and the other card move together; cards come back at once
=======================================================================================================
Base: 4.5.724. Off: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-sync725-off (= 4.5.724). Per-action crash latch on
sync725 only; kb707 itself is never latched. No new hooks, nothing new at load. Messages and
Beeper 684 keep 4.5.724's timing. Split / third stage unchanged.

WHY THE OTHER CARD WAS NOT IN SYNC (traced; no new log)
kb707 already moved the typing card and slid the other card in one spring block. The lag came from
WHEN it looked:
- It looked 0.05 s after a keyboard note and again at 0.40 s. The first look often saw the 243 pt
  frame (keyboard before its suggestion bar). The lift for 243 pt is smaller, the lifted card did not
  yet reach the other card, so only the typing card moved. At 0.40 s the 301 pt frame gave a bigger
  lift, the cards now overlapped, and the other card's slide started then: ~0.35 s late, as a second
  move (and the typing card jumped again).
- Coming back: after the keyboard left, kb707 waited 0.4 s with no keys + a 0.45 s re-check, and kept
  waiting while the keys were still sliding off (they count as "on screen"): 0.4 - 0.9 s late.
Not the cause: separate curves (both cards share one spring), another mover (none in single /
two-stage), layout re-applying the offset (kb720 keeps it), the status bar (does not move cards).

WHAT CHANGED
- On every keyboard note kb707 decides at once (next run-loop turn), not 0.05 s later (the 0.40 s
  follow-up check stays).
- A short pre-bar frame is planned with the full keyboard height already seen, so the final lift and
  the slide are known on the first look: one spring, same start, same duration for all cards.
- Keyboard will-hide / frame gone: a noted hide wins over keys still sliding off; 0.1 s blip guard (a
  show inside it cancels and nothing moves), then every card springs back in one shared animation.
  The 0.4 s absence wait and 0.45 s re-check are no longer used for this.
- Stacking order is still put back 0.45 s after the cards return.
