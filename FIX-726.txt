CAUSE
1. outside707 fired on a touch-down in the home-swipe band, 0.9 s after the user opened the app.
2. The card could not come back: a corner pull is the only way back, and it is refused while a previous
   corner pull is still registered (_systemPull) or when the pull starts in the corner opposite to the
   one the stage remembers. The 09:07:01 pull ran straight into a home swipe, so SpringBoard never sent
   its end; the stale pull then blocked every later pull (and heal / outside checks), with no log line.
   (corner707 side mix-ups after the home tuck's opposite-corner assignment can do the same.)
Not involved: sync725 (built later), kb707 offsets (cards spring back on "typing ended"), heal700.

FIX
outside726 (off: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-outside726-off):
- never in the bottom 140 pt of the screen (home swipe start);
- 56 pt around every card (was 24);
- never within 1 s of a touch the stage took (a card) or of a stage opening / picker showing / card
  closing;
- waits 0.45 s (was 0.18) and re-checks, so a swipe or drag that began is refused. (A true tap test would
  need a new touch hook; not added.)
restore726 (off: /var/mobile/.dynamicstage-restore726-off), every stage tick (1.5 s) while a card is
minimised:
- releases a corner pull SpringBoard never ended (no finger, not Began/Changed for > 1 s, or > 4 s);
- clears a corner-pull block set far in the future;
- makes the remembered corner side match the corner the minimised card is drawn in.
Each action is logged ("restore726 ...", once per 30 s per kind) with a per-action crash latch.
