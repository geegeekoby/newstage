DynamicStage 4.5.738 (on 4.5.737)

1. Crash: Logggy, build 4.5.735, SIGTRAP at 10:08:55 NZDT
Frame 1 (0x109a68658) is offset 0x8658 in the 735 binary, the instruction right
after "blraaz x8", the call to the original app-view settings update in the Live
(display mode 4) hook in Tweak.xm. The log line just before it, from the same
code, is "appview699 Live update passed to SpringBoard (mode=4 drag=0)". This is
SpringBoard's own update trapping, the known site from 669/697/704. That update
came about 1 s after a "stage display-layout change" hold, with a finger just down
on the rim ("rim651 hit main-outer-top at 57,463") before the drag was recognised.
Fix (live738): a Live update is now also held while a finger is on the rim or drag
shell (touch-down plus 1.5 s), and for 2 s after any other hold.
Off: -live738-off (per-action latch).

2. Drag lag and the grab rim (Slide log)
- Every pan frame rebuilt the card outline twice (CATransaction plus layer
  properties) and re-laid the 4 rim strips three times, even though only the
  shell moves. Now skipped when nothing changed (drag738, off: -drag738-off).
- Cause found in the audit (item 3a): features beyond the 32nd switch name were
  silently off. That can include drag706, perf706 and anim698 savings, depending
  on start order.
- Rim: a 420 pt card on a 430 pt screen leaves only 5 pt of rim at the left and
  right edges, and the Slide log has grabs there that never reached the rim
  ("system gesture held" with no hit). Where a card hugs the screen edge, the grab
  strip now covers 14 pt on screen, reaching at most 10 pt over the card's
  outermost edge column, and a drag can start there (rim738, off: -rim738-off).
  The rest of the card stays app-only (rim731).

3. Audit
a) Switch table overflow (a real bug): DS699_MAX was 32, but 44 feature names are
   in use. Names registered after the 32nd got no slot, so DSFeature699On returned
   NO and those features were silently off. Raised to 128.
b) Per-action crash latch: the -trying file was created on every action, which can
   mean main-thread disk I/O per frame. Now rewritten at most every 2 s; the 15 s
   clear still follows the latest action.
c) Wrongly latched features: the relay crashes latched off whatever had acted in
   the 15 s before each crash. The Slide log shows "phonesize694 squash LATCHED
   OFF". The first start of 738 clears the latch files written since 734 was
   installed (09:40 NZDT), once. Marker: -latch738-cleared.
d) No blocking calls (dispatch_sync, sleep, semaphore waits, waitUntilDone).
   Diagnostics writes run on a background queue.
e) Features without a latch: rim731, drag738 and rim738 only change hit areas and
   layout caching (no system calls); their off files work. Every other feature
   has a latch.
f) Private classes: the class lookups checked are messaged through the nil-safe
   objc runtime; selectors are checked with respondsToSelector before use.
g) Timers: the stuck727 / heal700 / kb707 / black731 checks all stand down for a
   finger, the keyboard, calls (call733) and the call screen.
h) Half-finished or risky: the app trace relay was removed in 736.

4. Beeper top bar: top737 removed
The band in IMG_4476 was the 690 copied top-bar strip. It only appears because the
694 squash had been latched off by the relay crash (item 3c). With the squash back,
the card shows Beeper's real top bar (settings cog included), with no strip.
top737's lower inset would have put Beeper's content under that real bar, so it is
gone.
