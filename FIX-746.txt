src746: root cause of the ghost keyboard "up" (Depd 12:53:57.494). The app
dylib's line "kb hide x=0 y=458 w=420 h=233" is parsed by
noteAppReportedKeyboardLine, which ignored the kind (show/hide) and judged by
geometry only, so a hide frame (card-local, keys at the card bottom) counted as
keys up. A "hide" line is now a hide and a card-local frame at the card's
height is gone. Every app-credited keyboard "up" is logged with its path
(src746 up ... via ...), visible keys, slot and time since the last hide.
ghost745 stays as the backstop. Off: .dynamicstage-src746-off (latch).
