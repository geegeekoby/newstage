lift756    One lift rule for every stage and app (Beeper, Messages, Messenger, all):
           the typed-in card sits just above the keys (moves up OR down), every other
           stage slides off to the side, all return together on dismiss.
           Removed exemptions: 755 lift755 (Beeper no-lift), 684/680 "card kept at
           rest", the "slides its own composer" top-card exemption, kb723's Beeper skip.
           Beeper's 684 card still uses its pinned keys top so its bar lines up.
           (Split / third-stage layouts keep their own mover.)
park756    Promoting the second card to primary (an app's card closed by opening it
           full screen) now carries its corner side and picture and hides the empty
           card's rim. 755: Beeper sat in the left corner but the stage thought
           "right", so it could not be pulled back, and the orphan top rim / outline
           stayed. A corner pull also matches a parked card by where it is drawn.
keys756    A card minimised while its app (no app dylib) was typing keeps the
           keyboard system's "on" with nothing drawn; tapping the field then shows
           nothing. When the card comes back and the app still owns the keyboard,
           the keyboard report is replayed: keys shown, card lifted.
type756    Typing owner cleared when its card is minimised or closed; the "ignored"
           line names the real typer.
track756   host750 logs when a different app moves into a slot (old app left).
coalesce756 The full-keyboard top is remembered per app (fixes the 324 -> 369 double lift).
beat756    Heartbeat every 30 s so a respring without SIGTERM reads as a clean exit.
